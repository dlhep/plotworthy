Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal data. Last updated: August 2026.

This policy explains what personal data Plotworthy ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and your rights. If you have any questions, contact us at hello@plotworthy.co.uk.

Who we are

Plotworthy provides property feasibility guidance and connects property owners with architects and builders. For the purposes of UK data protection law, we are the data controller for the personal data described below.

The data we collect

  • Information you give us through our forms — such as your name or business name, email address, the property address or postcode, and details about your project or the professional services you offer.
  • Technical and usage data collected automatically when you visit the site — such as your approximate location, device and browser type, and the pages you view — through privacy-focused website analytics.

How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the feasibility information or report you request.
  • To connect you, at your request, with relevant architects or builders.
  • To operate accounts and subscriptions for clients and professionals.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To send you service messages, and — only where you have agreed — occasional updates. You can opt out at any time.

Our legal basis

We rely on: your consent (for example, when you submit a form or opt in to updates); the performance of, or steps to enter into, a contract with you; and our legitimate interests in operating and improving our service, balanced against your rights.

Who we share it with

  • Architects and builders on our network — but only the details relevant to your project, and only when you ask to be connected.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, including our form-handling provider (Web3Forms), our hosting and analytics provider (Vercel), and our email provider. They act on our instructions.
  • Authorities or advisers where we are required to by law.

We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access your data; to have it corrected or deleted; to restrict or object to its processing; and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email hello@plotworthy.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies & analytics

We use privacy-focused analytics to understand site usage. We do not use advertising cookies. Where required, we will ask for your consent to non-essential cookies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version will always be posted on this page.

This policy is provided as a general starting template for the Plotworthy website and should be reviewed and tailored to the business's final data-processing arrangements (including any ICO registration and company details) before launch.
