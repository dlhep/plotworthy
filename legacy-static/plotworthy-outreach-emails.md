# Plotworthy — Professional Recruitment Outreach

Short, honest templates for inviting local architects and builders to join. Keep them personal — mention something specific about their work or area in the first line. People reply to messages that feel written for them, not blasted out.

Replace anything in **[square brackets]**.

## Sending tips

- **Personalise the first line.** One genuine, specific detail about their work or area lifts reply rates more than anything else.
- **Attach the one-pager** (the "Join Plotworthy" flyer, saved as a PDF) so they can see it at a glance.
- **Send from hello@plotworthy.co.uk** (or your own name) — a domain address looks credible.
- **Keep it short.** These are already about the right length; resist adding more.
- **Follow up once.** Most positive replies come on the second touch. One follow-up is plenty — don't chase harder than that.
- **Track who you've contacted** in a simple spreadsheet: name, company, area, date contacted, reply, status.
- **Be honest that it's early.** "Founding" framing is true and appealing — professionals like getting in first and shaping something.
