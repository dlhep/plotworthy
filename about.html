About

Property potential, decoded.

We help homeowners and developers understand what their property could become — and connect them to the right professionals to make it happen.

Why we started Plotworthy

Every day, people own — or are about to buy — a property that could be worth far more with a change of use, a conversion or a development. But most never move forward, because the planning system is opaque, professional advice is expensive to even start, and it's never clear who to call first. Plotworthy exists to remove those three barriers: give you clear, expert guidance on what's possible, then connect you to trusted local professionals when you're ready.

How we assess your property

Our feasibility guidance is grounded in real planning knowledge — use classes, permitted development, local plan policy and the practical factors councils actually weigh up, like parking, amenity and design. Your free snapshot gives you a fast, honest read on whether an idea is worth exploring; your paid report goes into the detail, sets out your options, and gives you indicative costs and value so you can make an informed decision.

How we vet the professionals

Relevant experience

Architects and builders are checked for experience relevant to the work — from change-of-use schemes to conversions and extensions.

Insurance & accreditations

Builders confirm their insurance and any accreditations, so you're dealing with credible, cover-backed firms.

A pre-qualified brief

Every professional receives your vetted brief up front, so they approach you already understanding the job.

Reviews over time

Client reviews build on the platform, so quality is visible and the best professionals rise to the top.

How we're different

Directories and lead sites hand you a list of names and leave you to work out what's even possible. Planning consultants can tell you what's possible, but they're expensive and slow to engage. Plotworthy sits in between: we start with clarity — a clear read on your property's potential — and only then connect you to professionals, each already briefed on your project. You get the insight of an expert and the convenience of a marketplace, without the cold-calling.
