How it works

From "I wonder if I could…" to a clear plan of action.

Most people sitting on a property with potential never move forward — because they don't know what's allowed, what it costs, or who to call first. Plotworthy removes all three unknowns.

STEP 01

Tell us about the property

Address, what you own or are thinking of buying, and what you're curious about — a change of use, an extension, splitting into flats, or a full development. Two minutes.

STEP 02

Get your feasibility report

Our experts assess what's realistically possible: planning route, opportunities, risks to be aware of, indicative costs and value. Start with a free snapshot, then go as deep as you need.

When you're ready, we introduce you to local architects and builders on our network who can quote for the work. You compare, you choose. No pressure, no lock-in.

Worked example

Turning a tired family home into a small care home (C3 → C2)

C2 "residential institution" schemes — care homes, children's homes, supported living — are in high demand, but every change to or from C2 needs full planning permission, with no shortcut. That's exactly where expert guidance pays for itself. Here's what a Plotworthy report unpacks.

What's possible
6-bed registered care use, subject to planning & CQC
The planning route
Full permission — no permitted development right applies
Watch-outs
Parking, neighbour amenity, highways, fire & care standards
The numbers
Indicative conversion cost & projected operating value
